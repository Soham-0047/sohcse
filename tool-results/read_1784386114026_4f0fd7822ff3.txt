Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs): MCQs questions are considered one of the most common questions in the GATE examination. Every question has four options, and the candidate needs to select one correct answer. The MCQs-type questions may test core concepts, definitions, engineering principles, their practical applications and reasoning. The MCQs-based questions test more about conceptual understanding rather than formulas and calculation skills. Multiple Select Questions : MSQs questions are also the common questions under the GATE examinations. For these questions, the candidate can select more than one option. The MSQs-based questions do not have negative marking, but every correct answer must be selected to receive full marks. MSQs questions demand a deeper understanding of engineering concepts because students must evaluate every option carefully. Numerical Answer Types: Under the Numerical Based questions, this involves questions which have calculations. These are based on conceptual understanding. Students need to enter the correct value without any options. Furthermore, you must have strong theoretical concepts; they will help in simplifying the NATs questions.
